# Legal templates — DRAFTS, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

These are **starting-point templates** for a workplace-monitoring product. They **must be
reviewed and finalized by a qualified lawyer** for your jurisdiction before use. The product
owner operates a legal practice (soniandsoni.legal) and should drive this.

Monitoring employees carries strict legal duties that vary by country/state. At minimum most
jurisdictions require:
- **Disclosure + consent**: employees must be told what is monitored, why, and agree.
- **Legitimate purpose + proportionality**: collect only what's needed (this product is metadata-
  first, no keystrokes/contents — keep it that way).
- **Data protection**: lawful basis, retention limits, access controls, breach handling
  (India **DPDP Act 2023**; **GDPR** for EU data subjects; US state laws vary).
- **No monitoring of truly private spaces/time** where prohibited.

Files:
- `EULA.md` — software licence between SNS (vendor) and the business (customer).
- `PRIVACY-POLICY.md` — how the business, as data controller, handles monitored data.
- `MONITORING-CONSENT.md` — the notice + acknowledgement each monitored employee signs.

Placeholders are in `{{BRACES}}`.
